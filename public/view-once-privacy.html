1 JaText View Once Protection

Why You Can Only View This Once

Some moments, verification codes, personal photos, voice notes, and sensitive thoughts are meant for a single conversation in the present, not an indefinite archive. JaText View Once combines end-to-end encryption with strict one-time viewing so private moments stay ephemeral.

How View Once Works on JaText

  • Encrypted before leaving the sender's device. Whether a View Once message contains text, a photo, an audio clip, or a combination of media and text, it is sealed with end-to-end encryption just like regular JaText messages.
  • Hidden in chat previews and bubbles. Before you open a View Once message, only "Click to view." appears in the thread so nearby onlookers cannot read sensitive content on your screen.
  • Permanent burn on exit. Opening a View Once message launches a dedicated fullscreen viewer. The moment you press Back or close the viewer with the X button, the message is permanently marked as opened across your account and local decrypted material is cleared. It cannot be reopened on mobile or linked desktop devices.

Minimize Digital Footprint

Every saved photo or text increases the risk of accidental exposure if a phone or laptop is ever left unlocked. Ephemeral messages shrink what remains in chat history.

Natural Human Conversation

Spoken conversations disappear as soon as they are heard. View Once restores that natural privacy to digital messaging without sacrificing clarity.

Zero-Knowledge Security

JaText servers never hold your private decryption keys. Paired with Two-Factor Authentication and Passkeys, your account and conversations remain under your control.

Share Thoughtfully

Only send View Once messages to people you trust. While JaText permanently locks a View Once message as soon as the viewer closes it and never saves View Once media to camera rolls automatically, no digital app can prevent someone from pointing a second physical camera at their screen.
