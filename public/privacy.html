Privacy Policy

For JaText · Effective date: 25 October 2026

JaText is a small, independent messaging app that lets people create an account, build a profile, add friends, and exchange messages and attachments. This policy explains what information JaText collects, why, and what happens to it. It is written in plain language and reflects how the app actually works.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you register with an email address and password, we store your email address and authentication credentials. Authentication is handled by our backend provider (Convex Auth); your password is not visible to other users.
  • Google sign-in information. If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us so we can create and sign you into your JaText account. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.
  • Profile information. Display name, username, biography, pronouns, profile picture, banner image, and avatar colour you choose to set. Your profile is visible to other JaText users (for example when they view your profile or add you as a friend).
  • Messages and attachments. The text messages you send and any files you attach are stored so they can be delivered to and displayed for the people you chat with. Messages are visible to the participants in that conversation.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and backend providers may record standard technical data (such as IP address and request logs) as part of running and securing the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and let you sign in.
  • To display your profile to other users of JaText.
  • To deliver your messages and attachments to your friends.
  • To keep the service running, secure, and free of abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your messages for advertising, and JaText does not show third-party ads.

3. Google user data

JaText's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information obtained through Google sign-in (name, email address, profile picture) is used only to create and authenticate your JaText account and to pre-fill your JaText profile. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties except the service providers listed below that are necessary to run JaText.

4. Service providers

  • Convex — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage, so account, profile, message, and attachment data is stored on Convex infrastructure.
  • Vercel — hosts the JaText website.
  • Google — provides optional sign-in if you choose it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your account data, profile, and messages are kept for as long as your account exists so the service can function. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the support email address shown on the Google sign-in consent screen and we will delete it.

6. Children

JaText is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required to hold an account in your country). Do not use JaText if you are under that age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the developer at the user support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen for JaText.
